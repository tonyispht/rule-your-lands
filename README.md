# Rule Your Lands

**RYL · A Dragon Rider's Realm**: a mobile-friendly 3D action RPG that runs in the browser.

▶️ **Play:** https://tonyispht.github.io/rule-your-lands/

- RYL2-style classes, subclasses, stats and skills
- Ember, your baby dragon companion and mount, plus horses, wolves and a sky griffin
- Expanding world with leveled zones, outposts, bosses and quests
- Specialties: herbalism, alchemy, mining and jewelcrafting; a personal market stall
- Land plots, homes and gardens
- Character customization, wearable effects, Fame ranks and guilds
- Serverless multiplayer (peer-to-peer, host-as-server)

Everything is in a single `index.html` file. Progress is saved in your browser.
